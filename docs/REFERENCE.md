# Film reference

The visual authority is the supplied Interstate 60 footage. Copyrighted clips and
annotated frames are not distributed with this repository. They are not required
to build or use the application.

The machine has a blue case, English labels, a question field, an eight-ray star,
an orange ball, colored pins, a spiral, an alternating indicator and YES/NO targets.
Keep the indicator independent of the answer. The tube stays neutral until the target.

Movie follows two authored trajectories; Freestyle computes new collisions while
retaining the film-inspired camera language. Both use one deterministic timeline.
Reference timings and perspective calibration are in references/film-calibration.json.

Reconstruction decisions must distinguish visible film evidence from assumptions.
Do not reproduce monitor blur, noise or cropped framing at the expense of usability.
