# Visual review criteria

Compare the complete sequence as well as fixed frames: composition, camera scale,
ball silhouette, pin contacts, spiral openings, target reveal and indicator rhythm.
Review both routes with YES and NO, on desktop and mobile.

Preserve the original before-state and film material privately. Existing comparison
pages require locally supplied media and do not ship in the production application.

Browser automation establishes behavior and rendering checks. It does not establish
artistic acceptance, exact film identity or performance on a physical phone.
